# Russian Tea with Tang

Ingredients
2 cups sugar
1 cup Tang orange powder
1/2 cup instant tea
1/2 tsp. cinnamon
1/2 tsp. cloves
1 package lemonade mix, 1 quart size

Instructions
Mix ingredients together and store in a zip lock bag.
Mix 2-3 tablespoons with a cup of hot water.
