# Quinoa Breakfast Bowl

#### Ingredients



  * 1 cup water

  * 1/2 cup quinoa

  * 1/2 cup unsweetened coconut milk

  * 1 handful of dried fruit

  * 2 tbsp pumpkin seeds

  * 1 handful of coconut chips

  * 2 tbsp of honey

#### Instructions



  1. Unpack and assemble MiniMo burner, and add 1 cup of water.

  2. Light MiniMo burner, and add 1/2 cup of quinoa.

  3. Stir and bring to a boil, then lower the temperature to a simmer, cover with lid and cook for 15 minutes.

  4. Add 1/2 cup of unsweetened coconut milk, 1 handful of dried fruit, 2 tbsp of pumpkin seeds, 1 handful of coconut chips, and 2 tbsp of honey.

  5. Stir together and enjoy.
