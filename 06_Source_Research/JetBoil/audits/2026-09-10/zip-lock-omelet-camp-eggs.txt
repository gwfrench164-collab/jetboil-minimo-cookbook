# Baggie Omelets - Camp Eggs

#### Ingredients



  * 2 Eggs

  * 1 Tbsp grated cheese

  * 1 Tbsp finely chopped green peppers

  * 1 Tbsp finely chopped onion

  * Salt and Pepper to taste

#### Instructions

  1. Fill cooking cup to the max safe line (1 cup) with water and bring to boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low.

  2. Place all ingredients in a Stasher snack bag and remove as much air from the bag as possible and zip close. Place bag in boiling water.

  3. After about 1 minute, remove bag from water and move mixture around in bag and place back in boiling water.

  4. Repeat about every minute until the eggs are cooked to your liking. (About 5 mins)

  5. Remove bag from Jetboil cooking system and serve immediately.
