# Hot Granola Breakfast

#### Ingredients

  * 1 cup of dried oats
  * 1 cup of water
  * 1 cup trail mix (I prefer raisins, cranberries, almonds, pumpkin seeds, papaya, pineapple, and coconut flakes)
  * 1/4 Tsp cinnamon
  * 1/4 Tsp of brown sugar

#### Instructions

  1. At home, in a Stasher bag, combine oats, trail mix, cinnamon, and brown sugar.  
  2. On the trail, bring 1 cup of water to a boil and add the pre-packed ingredients.  
  3. Boil for approximately 5 to 10 minutes, or until desired thickness is achieved.
  4. Serve immediately.
