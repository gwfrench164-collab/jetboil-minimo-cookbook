Source: [Jack Pine Jim's Power Brunch](https://jetboil.johnsonoutdoors.com/us/recipes/jack-pine-jims-power-brunch)

Published wording from the recorded source extract; only markup and outer whitespace are normalized.

### Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons of Irish oatmeal pellets, uncooked
- 6 tablespoons of dry-roasted Edamame (green soy beans) , lightly salted
- 3-5 tablespoons mixed dried berries
- One tablespoon cold-pressed flaxseed oil
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- Tabasco sauce

### Instructions

#### The Night Before

1. Place the oat pellets in your Jetboil with a cup of water just before you hit the sack. Let them soak all night. This cuts the cook time down from 30 minutes to 5.

#### In the Morning

2. Fire up your Jetboil and boil the oat pellets for three minutes.

3. Toss in the dry-roasted soy beans and the fruit, and simmer for two more minutes.

4. Add the brown sugar and flaxseed oil and let sit, covered, until cool enough to eat.

5. Add the Tabasco drops for a nice variation for lunch
