# Baggie Omelets - Camp Eggs

## Official Recipe

### Official Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 tablespoon grated cheese
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped green peppers
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped onion
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Official Instructions

1. Fill cooking cup to the max safe line with water and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to low.
2. Place all ingredients in a Stasher snack bag, remove as much air from the bag as possible, and zip close.
3. Place bag in boiling water.
4. After about 1 minute, remove bag from water, move mixture around in bag, and place back in boiling water.
5. Repeat about every minute until the eggs are cooked to your liking, about 5 minutes.
6. Remove bag from JetBoil cooking system and serve immediately.

## MiniMo Adaptation

For the MiniMo, do **not** treat the SUMO-style fill guidance as the practical working level.

Use the MiniMo approximately **half full** with water. Filling too high can boil over.

## Field-Tested Variations

These are **not part of the official JetBoil recipe**.

Useful additions:

- Chopped hot dogs
- Chopped ham
- Cooked bacon
- Sausage crumbles
- Mushrooms
- Spinach

## MiniMo Tips

- Keep the water level conservative.
- Reduce heat once boiling.
- Monitor closely for boil-over.
- Use a utensil or tongs to remove the hot bag.
- Avoid letting the bag rest directly against excessively hot metal above the waterline.

## Notes

The previous project file incorrectly treated chopped ham as if it belonged in the main ingredient list. Ham belongs under field-tested variations, not the official recipe.
