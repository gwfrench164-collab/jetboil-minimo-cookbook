Source: [French Toast Recipe](https://jetboil.johnsonoutdoors.com/us/recipes/french-toast)

Published wording from the recorded source extract; only markup and outer whitespace are normalized.

### Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 3 drops of vanilla
- 2 TBSP of butter
- 3 slices of bread
- Maple syrup
- Powdered sugar
- Cinnamon

### Instructions

1. Unpack and assemble MiniMo burner with Pot Support, and your Summit Skillet.

2. Crack and whisk 3 eggs, then add 3 drops of vanilla.

3. Light MiniMo burner and add 2 tbsp of butter.

4. When hot, dip bread in mixture and place on skillet.

5. Cook until golden brown on each side, and serve with maple syrup, powdered sugar and cinnamon.

### Featured Recommended Products

- MiniMo Cooking System
