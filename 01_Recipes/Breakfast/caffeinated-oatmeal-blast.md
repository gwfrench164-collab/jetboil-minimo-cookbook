## Official Recipe

Source: [Caffeinated Oatmeal Blast](https://jetboil.johnsonoutdoors.com/us/recipes/caffeinated-oatmeal-blast)

Published wording from the recorded source extract; only markup and outer whitespace are normalized.

### Ingredients

- 1 package Good To-Go oatmeal
- 1 ¼ cups water
- 3 tablespoons of ground coffee
- 1 tablespoon chopped chocolate-covered espresso beans
- 1 tablespoon granola
- 1 teaspoon cacao nibs
- ½ teaspoon flax seeds

### Tools

- MicroMo, Flash, or Zip stove system
- Jetboil French Press
- Spoon

### Instructions

#### Before you leave for your adventure:

1. Chop chocolate-covered espresso beans and then place them in a small reusable bag (or another sturdy container) along with granola, cacao nibs, and flax seeds.

#### When you're ready to eat:

2. Unpack and assemble a coffee-press compatible Jetboil (the MicroMo, Flash, or Zip all fit the regular-sized coffee press).

3. Bring 1 ¼ cups of water to a boil, then add in coffee grounds. Stir gently to mix in coffee, then cover with the Jetboil french press plunger and let sit for 5 minutes.

4. While coffee is brewing, open the Good To-Go oatmeal package and remove the oxygen absorber.

5. Once your coffee is ready, gently push the french press plunger down to capture the coffee grounds. Immediately pour the freshly brewed coffee into the oatmeal package. Stir and let sit for five minutes.

6. Finally, top your caffeinated oatmeal with the mix of espresso beans, granola, cacao nibs, and flax seeds that you prepared before your trip.

7. Mix, enjoy, and let the coffee-fueled adventures begin!

## MiniMo Adaptation

Editorial notes; not official recipe text and not field-tested.

The recipe specifies MicroMo, Flash or Zip with the regular-sized press. MiniMo compatibility must be handled separately: Jetboil lists the Grande Coffee Press for MiniMo. Use a press confirmed to fit your pot.

Keep the two five-minute waits in the instructions. The article introduction claims six minutes, which conflicts with those sequential waits; do not substitute the previous eight-minute estimate.

Preserve the package preparation and oxygen-absorber removal step. Changing to a different oatmeal brand requires its own hydration instructions and is not the official recipe.

Equipment sequence: the MiniMo manual requires ignition with no pot or skillet on the burner, followed by careful placement of cookware. Follow that manual sequence wherever the recipe wording is abbreviated or ambiguous.
