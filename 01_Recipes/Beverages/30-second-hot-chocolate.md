## Official Recipe

Source: [30 Second Hot Chocolate](https://jetboil.johnsonoutdoors.com/us/recipes/30-second-hot-chocolate)

Published wording from the recorded source extract; only markup and outer whitespace are normalized.

### Ingredients

- 1/3 cup cocoa
- 1 1/3 cup instant nonfat dry milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons powdered non-dairy creamer
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Optional: instant coffee

### Instructions

#### Preparation at Home:

1. Mix all ingredients and store in a zip lock baggie.

#### Drinking on the Trail:

2. Use your Jetboil Flash Cooking System to heat water.

3. To one cup hot water, add 1/3 cup dry mix (about four to six heaping tablespoons) and stir well. Add a little instant coffee for mocha. Life is good.
